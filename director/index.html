How to direct a disaster

Each turn is one move, with no timer while you decide. Pick one action to manage the town’s delivery queue, protect private parcels, or help the crew. The storm’s next event is the same in both futures.

When you’re ready, hit Rewind & fork future to play the same turns again. Compare the two runs to see how your choices changed the outcome.

Keys: 1–4 choose an action · R rewinds to replay. You can also load a scenario JSON from your device.
